1. Introduction

CommercialTech ("we", "our", or "us") provides business software for property management, construction, cleaning, maintenance, security and service companies. This includes the CommercialTech mobile application, the OpsNexus Field mobile application, and the web platform (together, our "products"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you use our products.

Our products are used by the staff of business customers. Your employer or organization (the company that gave you your login) decides how the product is used and is responsible for the staff information it records. We provide the software and process that information on your organization's behalf.

By using our products, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the product and the features your organization uses, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, job position/department, and your organization's name
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates when you use features such as check-in, clock in/out, patrol checkpoints or incident reports (only when you actively use them)
  • Photos: Images you take or upload for task documentation, incident reports, and (in OpsNexus Field) your attendance selfie
  • Usage Data: Task updates, attendance records, leave and other requests, messages sent within the platform
  • Device Information: Device type and operating system; for the CommercialTech app, a device token for push notifications

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide our services to your organization
  • To record attendance and GPS check-in/check-out times
  • To let your organization assign, track and review work, and to generate reports
  • To send push notifications about tasks, emergencies, and announcements (CommercialTech app)
  • To enable communication between staff members
  • To improve our platform and fix issues

4. Camera & Photo Access

Our apps request access to your device camera, and the CommercialTech app also to your photo library, for the following purposes:

  • Taking photos to document completed tasks
  • Uploading evidence photos for emergency and incident reports
  • Uploading profile photos and building logos (CommercialTech app)
  • Taking an attendance selfie and completing the live face check (OpsNexus Field, described in section 12)

Photos are only taken or accessed when you explicitly choose to take or upload a photo. We never access your camera or photos without your action.

5. Location Data

Location data is only collected when you actively use a feature that needs it, such as tapping "Check In" / "Check Out", clocking in or out, or submitting a patrol or incident report. We do not track your location continuously or in the background. Location data is used for attendance verification and for your organization's site records.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Railway cloud infrastructure with PostgreSQL databases. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • JWT token authentication
  • Encrypted passwords using bcrypt
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Rate limiting to prevent unauthorized access
  • On OpsNexus Field, your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage until you sign out

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising. Your data is only shared:

  • Within your organization (managers, HR and staff who are allowed to see it)
  • With Railway for cloud hosting and database storage
  • With Cloudflare (R2 storage) for photo storage in OpsNexus Field (photos only)
  • With Cloudinary for photo storage in the CommercialTech app (photos only)
  • With Expo/Google for push notifications in the CommercialTech app (device tokens only)
  • If the law requires us to disclose it

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your organization maintains an active account with CommercialTech. For OpsNexus Field, attendance records and attendance selfie photos are kept while you are employed and for 12 months afterwards (or 12 months after your organization's account ends), and are then deleted. Upon account termination, other data is permanently deleted within 30 days upon request.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Because your organization controls the staff records in our products, you can also ask your organization's HR or administrator. We will pass on requests that belong to them. You can also write to us using the contact details below.

10. Children's Privacy

Our products are professional B2B platforms intended for use by employees 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes through the app or email. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. OpsNexus Field (staff mobile app)

This section applies to the OpsNexus Field mobile app for iOS and Android, used by staff of companies that use OpsNexus. It adds to the general terms above; where they differ, this section applies to OpsNexus Field.

Attendance (clock in / clock out)

When you clock in or out, the app records:

  • the date and time (taken from our server, not your phone), and whether you were on time, late or left early;
  • a selfie photo taken at that moment;
  • whether the live face check was completed (a yes/no result);
  • your GPS location (coordinates and accuracy) and your distance from your assigned work site, so your organization can confirm you were at the right place.

Live face check

Before a clock-in or clock-out the app asks you to look at the camera, blink and turn your head. This happens on your phone in real time (using Google ML Kit). The app only checks that a live person is present. It does not create a face template, does not identify who you are, and does not send video anywhere. Only the single selfie photo and the yes/no result are uploaded, and the selfie is available to your organization's HR for review.

Other information you submit

Tasks and comments, photos you attach to a task (up to six per task), work orders, patrol checkpoints and incident reports (including photos and, where used, your location), and requests such as leave, missed punch and overtime with the dates and reasons you enter. Your chosen language and light/dark theme are stored on your phone.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — for the face check and selfie, and to take photos of work and incidents.
  • Location (while using the app) — to confirm you are at your work site when you clock in or out, and for patrol and incident reports.

You can turn these off in your phone's settings, but clock-in will not work without them.

What OpsNexus Field does not do

  • It does not use the microphone or record audio or video.
  • It does not read your contacts, messages or photo gallery.
  • It does not track your location in the background.
  • It contains no advertising and does not sell your information.

Who can see it, and for how long

Managers and HR at your organization who are permitted to view attendance, tasks and requests can see this information. Photos are stored with Cloudflare R2 and the rest of the data with Railway, as described in section 7. Attendance records and selfies are kept as described in section 8 (while you are employed and for 12 months afterwards).
